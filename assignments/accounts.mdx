---
title: "Accounts"
description: "The companies you sell to and support, synced from your CRM"
---

An **account** is a company you sell to and support. Accounts come from your CRM, along with their fields, such as revenue, renewal date, plan and owner.

Trig keeps them in sync with your CRM on a schedule. Change a field in the CRM and Trig picks it up on the next sync. Accounts sit under Audience in the app, with the Books that group them.

When an account has an ID for another tool in your CRM, such as a Zendesk organization ID, Trig uses it to match the account in that tool. You can approve which ID columns Trig imports. {/* review: today this is done in chat with `approve_crm_id_column`, is there a screen? */}
