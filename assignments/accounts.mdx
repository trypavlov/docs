---
title: "Accounts"
description: "The companies you sell to and support, synced from your CRM or data warehouse"
---

An **account** is a company you sell to and support. Accounts come from a scheduled import from your CRM or data warehouse, along with their fields, such as revenue, renewal date, plan and owner.

Trig keeps them in sync on a schedule. Change a field at the source and Trig picks it up on the next sync. Accounts sit under Audience in the app, with the Books that group them.

When an account has an ID for another tool in your CRM, such as a Zendesk organization ID, Trig uses it to match the account in that tool.

If you want to use a field and can't find it, it hasn't been imported yet. Tell your Trig contact the field and where it lives, and it can be added to the import.
