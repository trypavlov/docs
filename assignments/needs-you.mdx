title: "Needs you"
description: "What Trig needs a person for"
---

**Needs you** lists what Trig needs a person for. Each item says what happened and what you can do next.

| Item | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| At-risk account | An account in one of your Books moved away from normal in a way that puts it at risk. |
| Connection lost | A CRM integration or agent toolkit stopped working. Reconnect it to keep assignments and metrics running. |
| Action failed | An assignment's action didn't run, such as a message that couldn't post. |
| Email not sent | A queued email has no sender, or Gmail refused it. |
| Account matching | Trig couldn't match an account to its record in another tool. |
| Core attributes | A field Trig relies on, such as the owner or renewal date, is missing or unclear. | {/* review: the labels and descriptions customers see for each kind */}

Some items open a conversation with Trig so you can sort them out in chat.
