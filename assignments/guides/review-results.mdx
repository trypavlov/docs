## Read a result

Open the assignment to see its last run. Each account lands in one group. The plan names the groups it looks for, such as "At risk" or "Renewal on track", most serious first. Every assignment also has three groups of its own.

| Group | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A group the plan looks for | The account met that group's condition. | Open it and read the reason. |
| Nothing to say | Trig checked the account and none of the plan's groups applied. | Spot check two or three. |
| Not enough facts | Trig couldn't establish the facts that decide between the groups. | Check the sources the assignment reads. |
| Gathering data | This is the first run, so there is nothing to compare with yet. | Wait for the next run. |

<Tip>
Spot check every group on the first few runs. An assignment that flags the right accounts can still be wrong about the ones it leaves out.
</Tip>

## Start each day from Needs you

Accounts an assignment flags, connections that stopped working and emails that couldn't send all appear in **Needs you** on the home page. Once assignments are live, start each day there. See [Needs you](/assignments/needs-you).

## Approve drafted emails

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Your emails">
    Drafted emails wait in **Your emails** in the side navigation. On an assignment, the **Emails** section opens the ones it drafted. Admins can also review every email queued across the company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read each draft">
    Check it against the account and the person it's for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve, edit or reject it">
    Reject anything addressed to the wrong person or giving the wrong reason, and tell your Trig contact. That points to a problem in the assignment as well as the draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve in batches">
    Once you trust the assignment on that Book, use **Approve and send all**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
An email an assignment drafts reaches a customer after someone on your team approves it.
</Note>

## Chat or assignment

Use the chat for one-off questions about an account or a Book, such as "What changed for this account in the last month?" Use an assignment for anything you want to happen again. See [Ask Trig](/assignments/ask-trig).
