A **metric** is a measure tracked against a Book to learn what normal looks like for that group, such as weekly active users, open support tickets or meetings in the last 30 days.

## Where metrics come from

Add an agent toolkit to choose where Trig finds metrics for these accounts, like product usage, support tickets or meeting activity. Trig learns what normal looks like for this Book and spots the accounts that move away from it.

## Set up metrics

1. Open the Book and add the sources the metrics come from.
2. Describe what matters in plain English, one metric at a time.
3. Trig builds each metric and tests it on a few accounts. Check the results against accounts you know.
4. Confirm each metric that is right, and correct or drop any that is not.
5. Choose daily or weekly measurement and activate.

Agree the metrics for a Book with your Trig contact before activating. Measurement setup is fixed once it starts. Trends appear after several runs, so a weekly metric takes weeks to show movement, and a daily one shows a trend in days.
