---
title: "Glossary"
description: "The names Trig uses"
---

| Name | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Account | A company you sell to and support. |
| User | Someone at your company who logs in to Trig. |
| Book | A saved group of accounts and the people in them. Assignments run against a Book, and metrics are tracked against it. |
| Audience | The section of the app that holds accounts, with the Books that group them. |
| Metric | A measure tracked against a Book to learn what normal looks like for that group. |
| Integration | A system Trig syncs with on its own because it needs it to run. Nearly always a CRM. |
| Agent toolkit | A tool or app Trig's agents can use to do work, such as a CRM, meeting transcripts, a support desk or Google Docs. |
| Assignment | A piece of work Trig does across a Book's accounts, once or on a schedule. |
| Needs you | The list of things Trig needs a person for. |
| Schedule | When or how often something runs. |
