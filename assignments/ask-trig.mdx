---
title: "Ask Trig"
description: "What you can ask Trig in the chat"
---

Trig has a chat in the app's sidebar and in Slack. Each person has their own Trig Agent, and it remembers your conversations across the app and Slack.

You can ask Trig to:

- find accounts by any CRM field or metric, such as "accounts renewing next month with falling usage"
- explain why an account landed in an assignment's result
- look something up in a connected toolkit, such as an account's open tickets
- connect a toolkit
- suggest an assignment and open it ready to plan
- build a report that updates on a schedule
- explain how anything in Trig works

## Setting up with Trig

While your workspace isn't set up, Trig offers the next step after answering your question. It shows you what each change will do and asks before it makes it. Say "not now" and it stops offering that step. Setup help appears in the app chat for admins and operations users.
