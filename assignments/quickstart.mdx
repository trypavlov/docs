---
title: "Set up your workspace"
description: "The five steps that set up a workspace"
---

A workspace is set up once it has five things. Your Trig contact works through them with you. You can do them in any order, but each one makes the next more useful.

1. **Connect your CRM.** Trig imports your accounts and who owns each one. With HubSpot or Salesforce, Trig also drafts a Book for each account owner. It connects on Settings › Integrations.
2. **Connect an agent toolkit.** Toolkits let Trig reach the tools your team uses, such as a support desk, meeting transcripts or billing.
3. **Measure a Book.** Add a toolkit as a source on a Book's Metrics page and describe what matters. Trig builds each metric and tests it on a few accounts, and you confirm the ones that are right.
4. **Put a Book live.** Once a Book is live, Trig reads its accounts and the review appears on the Book's page.
5. **Approve an assignment.** Describe the work, click **Plan it out**, read the plan, then approve it. Approving the plan makes the assignment live.

Each step shows something new: your accounts in Trig, the tools Trig can reach, how each account is doing, your own Book, and the first piece of work Trig does for you.
