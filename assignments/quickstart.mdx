---
title: "Set up your workspace"
description: "The five steps that set up a workspace"
---

A workspace is set up once it has five things. You can do them in any order, but each one makes the next more useful. Trig can walk you through every step in the chat: open it and ask "help me set up".

1. **Connect your CRM.** Trig imports your accounts and who owns each one. With HubSpot or Salesforce, Trig also drafts a Book for each account owner. Go to Settings › Integrations, or ask Trig to connect it.
2. **Connect an agent toolkit.** Toolkits let Trig reach the tools your team uses, such as a support desk, meeting transcripts or billing. Trig suggests toolkits from the ID columns it finds in your CRM. {/* review: the suggestion only appears in chat today */}
3. **Keep a metric.** Pick a toolkit as a source on a Book's Metrics page. Trig tests a few metrics on some of your accounts and you keep the ones you want. This takes a few minutes.
4. **Put a Book live.** Once a Book is live, Trig reads its accounts and the review appears on the Book's page.
5. **Start an assignment.** Describe the work, check the pages Trig writes for a few trial accounts, then start it.

Each step shows something new: your accounts in Trig, the tools Trig can reach, how each account is doing, your own Book, and the first piece of work Trig does for you.
