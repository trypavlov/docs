---
title: "What Trig does"
description: "Books, metrics, assignments and Needs you, and how they fit together"
---

Trig is an AI agent platform for account teams. It watches the accounts in your CRM, learns what normal looks like for each group of accounts, and does work across them on a schedule.

You work with four things:

- **Books** group your accounts, such as each person's own accounts or every account renewing this quarter.
- **Metrics** are the measures Trig tracks for a Book, synced from the tools your team already uses. Trig learns what normal looks like for the Book and spots the accounts that move away from it.
- **Assignments** are pieces of work Trig does across a Book's accounts, once or on a schedule. An assignment reads your tools, writes a page for each account, and can draft emails or post messages.
- **Needs you** lists what needs a person: an at-risk account, a connection that stopped working, or an action that didn't run.

Trig gets your accounts from your CRM, and it reaches your other tools through agent toolkits. [Your CRM and agent toolkits](/assignments/connections) explains the difference.

You can ask Trig about any of this in the chat. See [Ask Trig](/assignments/ask-trig).
