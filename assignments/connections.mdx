title: "Your CRM and agent toolkits"
description: "What your CRM does, and what agent toolkits do"
---

Trig connects to your tools in two ways, and each does a different job.

**Your CRM** is where your accounts come from. Trig syncs it on a schedule. Fields on each account, such as the owner, become ways to build your Books.

**Agent toolkits** let Trig's agents use your other tools, such as a support desk, meeting transcripts or Google Docs. Assignments use them to do their work. Trig can also sync metrics from a toolkit every day and show them in your Books. You choose which actions each toolkit allows. A toolkit can be shared with the workspace or kept to yourself.

**One tool can play more than one part.** If your CRM is HubSpot, Salesforce or Attio, connect it as an agent toolkit too when you want assignments to read or update it.

## Shared and personal toolkits

A shared toolkit works for everyone in the workspace. A personal toolkit uses your own sign-in, so it acts as you. An assignment runs as the person who created it, with their connections. If you start an assignment a teammate created, it uses their toolkits.

## Where to find them

Both live on Settings › Integrations. CRM integrations come first and agent toolkits second. You can also ask Trig to connect a tool from the chat.
