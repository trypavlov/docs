---
title: "Books"
description: "Saved groups of accounts that assignments and metrics work on"
---

A **Book** is a saved group of accounts. Assignments run against a Book, and metrics are tracked against it.

Add a Book and Trig compares every account in it with what normal looks like. It catches risks like falling usage before a renewal, and opportunities like an account nearing its seat limit.

## Which accounts are in a Book

Filter to the accounts that belong in it, such as "owner is Jane" or "renews in the next 90 days". Accounts that match later are added automatically. Filters use your CRM fields and your synced metrics.

When your CRM is connected, Trig drafts one Book for each account owner. The drafts aren't live until you put them live.

## Live Books

Once a Book is live, Trig reads its accounts and the review appears on its page. A Book's schedule sets how often Trig reviews and summarises its accounts.

## Common questions

- **Can a Book filter on data from a toolkit, such as Intercom?** Sync the value as a metric first. Books filter on CRM fields and on synced metrics.
- **Who can put a Book live?** The person who created it. {/* review: confirm this */}
